How to play
  1. One player creates a room and shares the code (up to 10 players).
  2. When everyone's in, the host starts the game. Each player gets a secret character.
  3. You can see everyone's character but your own.
  4. Players take 30-second turns. On your turn, ask the others yes/no questions, then type a guess.
  5. Guess right and you're out — you win! A wrong guess ends your turn.
  6. The game ends when only one player is left.
